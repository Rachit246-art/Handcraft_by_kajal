THE ARTIST - Kajal

Hi Am Kajal, the artist in bangalore behind Handcrafted by Kajal, specialises in creating soulful, handcrafted artworks that connect deeply with people and spaces. Every piece is carefully designed and individually created, ensuring no two artworks are ever the same.

Blending traditional techniques with contemporary creativity, Kajal transforms blank surfaces into meaningful visual experiences whether through murals, devotional creations, textured art, or personalized commissions.

Each artwork is not just made to be seen, but to be felt, remembered, and cherished for years to come.
