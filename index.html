THE ARTIST - KAJAL

Some people see a blank wall. A plain saree. An empty canvas.

I see a story waiting to be painted.

Hi, I'm Kajal Ankush — the artist behind Handcrafted by Kajal, a mixed media art studio based in Bangalore.

I specialize in creating original, soulful, hand-painted artworks that transform spaces, preserve memories, and tell stories that last forever. Every piece I create is made entirely by hand: no prints, no machines, no shortcuts—ensuring that no two artworks are ever the same.

From custom portraits and wall murals to hand-painted fabric art, devotional paintings, resin art, and mixed media creations—I work across every medium and every surface to bring your vision to life exactly as you imagined it.

With over 500 commissions delivered across India, for homes, offices, weddings, gifting, and special occasions—every piece I create carries the same intention it always has. To make something that is not just beautiful. But deeply personal. Completely yours and made to be cherished forever.
