The Legacy of Handcrafting

Every stroke, every pigment, and every canvas tells a unique story that mass production can never replicate. Handcrafting is the purest expression of the human soul.

About me

My journey as an artist began through fashion design, but over the years, art became much more than a profession for me it became a way of expressing emotions, stories, and creativity beyond words. After pursuing fashion designing at JD Institute of Fashion Technology and completing my advanced painting certification from Chitra Kala Parishad, I gradually evolved into a mixed media artist, exploring different forms of art and handcrafted creations.

For the past 5 years, I have been passionately creating artworks that blend texture, color, emotion, and imagination. My work includes texture art, resin artworks, devotional art, landscapes, custom portraits, wall murals, fabric art, handcrafted décor, and personalized artistic products. Over the years, I have had the opportunity to showcase my artworks at renowned exhibitions and galleries including Venkatappa Art Gallery, CKP Gallery, India Art Festival, Chitra Santhe, and the Indian Institute of World Culture. These experiences helped me connect with fellow artists, art lovers, and collectors who appreciate meaningful and expressive creations.

I have also conducted multiple art workshops where I share my techniques, creativity, and love for art with aspiring artists and enthusiasts. Today, through every artwork and handcrafted creation, I aim to bring emotions, beauty, and individuality to life turning simple ideas into meaningful artistic experiences.

The Handcrafting Process

01

Conceptualization

Sketching the raw idea on paper and understanding the deep emotional resonance behind the requested piece.

02

Underpainting

Laying down the tonal foundation on premium canvas to set the lighting, mood, and depth of the painting.

03

Rich Layering

Building vibrant textures using pure oil paints. Each layer must dry before the next is applied, requiring immense patience.

04

Varnishing

Sealing and protecting the artwork to ensure colors remain vibrant for generations to come, securing its legacy.
